<span class="anchor" id="about-me"></span>

**Long He**, Ph.D., is an Associate Professor at Zhengzhou University. Supported by the China Scholarship Council, he began a sponsored research visit to the Singapore University of Technology and Design in February 2025. He received his Ph.D. in Computer Application Technology from Jilin University in July 2026, his master's degree in Computer Science and Technology from Jilin University in 2023, and his bachelor's degree from the College of Computer Science and Technology, Chengdu University of Technology, in 2019. His honors include Jilin University’s First-Class Graduate Excellence Scholarship, First-Class Academic Achievement Scholarship, and the National Scholarship for Doctoral Students.

Over the past five years, he has published four papers as **first or corresponding author** in leading journals and international conferences, including IEEE TMC, IEEE/ACM ToN, IEEE WCM, and IEEE INFOCOM. He holds five granted invention patents, leads one Jilin University Graduate Innovation Research Program project, and has participated as a key contributor in two NSFC Young Scientists Fund projects (Category C) and two NSFC General Program projects.
